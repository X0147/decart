OBS Studio & NDI Integration Guide

1

Configure Input (OBS Virtual Camera)

Turn on OBS Virtual Camera in OBS Studio. In the settings sidebar of this web app, select "OBS Virtual Camera" as the video source to process your OBS scene output.

2

Capture Transformed Output

To bring the AI transformed feed back into OBS, add a Browser Source in OBS with this app's URL. Alternatively, click the Fullscreen button on the transformed card and use Window Capture in OBS.

3

Broadcast via DistroAV NDI

With the DistroAV plugin installed in OBS, go to Tools > DistroAV NDI Settings, enable the Main Output, and broadcast your AI styled stream to any NDI device or receiver on your network with zero latency!
